# Core Stance

## Evidence Before Narrative

- User artifacts and currently applicable rules outrank templates and heuristics.
- A claim must point to supplied data, a verifiable source, a reproduced result, or an explicit limitation.
- Keep uncertainty visible through the evidence states in `SKILL.md`.
- If the evidence cannot support the requested strength, narrow the claim before improving the prose.
- A research story is an ordering and explanation of evidence, not a replacement for missing experiments, citations, or attribution.

## Source Fidelity

- Trace specific facts, numbers, mechanisms, examples, and applications to a source or label them `proposed`/`inferred`.
- Keep vague inputs vague. Do not turn “potential use” into a named application or “promising” into a measured benefit.
- Distinguish reported results from locally reproduced results.
- Verify citations with a stable locator before treating them as support.

## Inheritance And Change Ledger

For reused research components, record:

| Component | Source | License/permission | Original role | Actual change | New evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not call unchanged inherited work a new contribution. A new name, wrapper, or combination does not by itself create novelty.

## Terminology Ledger

Lock model names, datasets, metrics, modules, abbreviations, notation, and translated terms before multi-section drafting. Prefer scientific consistency over lexical variety.

## AI Assistance Boundary

AI may organize, critique, translate, draft from supplied evidence, and propose experiments. It must not invent the research record. Mark AI involvement when the user's institution, venue, or applicable policy requires disclosure.

## Academic Integrity

- Attribute ideas, code, data, formulas, figures, wording, and modules.
- Preserve material failed runs, unstable deltas, and limitations.
- Do not recommend unfair comparison, selective omission of material counterevidence, plagiarism, duplicate submission, authorship misrepresentation, or detection evasion.
- When rules are time-sensitive, verify the current institutional, venue, data, and license requirements.
