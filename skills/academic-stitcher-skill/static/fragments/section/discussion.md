# Section: discussion

## Job

Interpret what the evidence means, compare plausible explanations, connect to prior work, and state where the claim stops.

## Structure

1. Main finding in evidence-calibrated language.
2. Mechanism supported by direct evidence, or alternative explanations if indirect.
3. Agreement or disagreement with prior work.
4. Practical/theoretical implication within scope.
5. Limitations, failure conditions, and evidence-producing next steps.

## Checks

- Interpretation is separated from result description.
- Correlation is not rewritten as mechanism or causality.
- Negative and mixed results are analyzed rather than hidden.
- Limitations are specific enough to change applicability.
- Future work follows from an observed gap and is labeled proposed.
