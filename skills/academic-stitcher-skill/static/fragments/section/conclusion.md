# Section: conclusion

## Job

State what was done, what the evidence supports, why it matters within scope, and what remains unresolved.

## Structure

1. Problem and actual contribution.
2. Key evidence and conditions.
3. Bounded implication.
4. Limitation or evidence-producing next step when appropriate.

## Checks

- No new data, experiments, citations, or claims.
- Contributions are stated as validated, mixed, preliminary, or proposed according to evidence.
- The conclusion does not broaden scope beyond the abstract and experiments.
- Future work is not used to disguise an unsupported current claim.
- Consistency matters more than forced paraphrase of the abstract.
