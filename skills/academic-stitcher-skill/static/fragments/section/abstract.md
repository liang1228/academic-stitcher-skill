# Section: abstract

## Job

Compress problem, specific gap, approach, strongest material evidence, contribution, and boundary.

## Structure

1. Context needed to understand the problem.
2. Specific unresolved gap supported by the literature or supplied evidence.
3. Approach and mechanism at the minimum useful level.
4. Main result with dataset/population, metric, comparator, and uncertainty when available.
5. Bounded implication or limitation.

## Evidence-State Handling

- With complete results, report the strongest decision-relevant result and any material counterevidence needed to interpret it.
- With mixed results, state where the method helps and where it does not.
- With no results, produce a proposal abstract labeled as such; do not write “experiments demonstrate”.

## Checks

- No invented numbers, datasets, mechanisms, applications, or novelty.
- No vague “excellent performance” in place of evidence.
- “State of the art” requires a current, defined comparison set and compatible protocol.
- The abstract and conclusion may share facts but should serve different rhetorical jobs, not use forced synonym replacement.
