# Section: related-work

## Job

Establish lineage, comparable alternatives, and the unresolved gap without paper-by-paper dumping or exaggerated criticism.

## Structure

1. Scope and organizing question.
2. Task/baseline lineage.
3. Relevant method or module families.
4. Closest prior work and exact overlap.
5. Evidence-based distinction and remaining gap.

## Working Matrix

| Source | Task/setting | Method family | Evidence | Limitation in scope | Relation to this work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Checks

- Organize by argument or method family, not citation order.
- Include close and contrary work, not only papers that make the proposed method look favorable.
- Attribute inherited modules explicitly.
- Separate a source's stated limitation from the author's inference.
- Do not claim novelty from search absence alone.
