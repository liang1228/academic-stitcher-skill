# Section: method

## Job

Explain the inherited system, actual delta, interface, and mechanism with enough detail to understand and reproduce the procedure.

## Structure

1. Problem formulation and assumptions.
2. Inherited baseline and cited components.
3. Overall data/procedure flow.
4. Each changed component: input, operation, output, interface, and rationale.
5. Objective, training/inference, sampling, or analysis procedure.
6. Implementation, complexity, permissions, and boundary conditions.

## Checks

- Define symbols and terms before use.
- Cite standard or inherited components rather than rewriting them as original.
- A mechanism statement predicts a measurable outcome; otherwise label it a hypothesis.
- Architecture figures and formulas reflect the actual implementation.
- Renaming, rearranging, or wrapping a component does not itself establish contribution.
