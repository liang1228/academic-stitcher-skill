# Section: introduction

## Job

Move from a sourced field problem to an unresolved gap, then show why the study is a justified next step.

## Funnel

1. Field/task and stakes, with citations for non-obvious claims.
2. What current approaches achieve.
3. The precise failure mode or unresolved question.
4. Why that gap matters under the study's setting.
5. Proposed move and predicted mechanism.
6. Contribution preview tied to available or planned evidence.
7. Scope and boundary.

## Contribution Test

For each contribution, map:

`problem -> actual change -> predicted/observed effect -> evidence -> boundary`

Do not invent a problem after selecting a borrowed module. Do not imply that close work is absent unless the literature search supports it.

## Checks

- Every specific trend, application, or prior-work claim has support.
- Problems correspond to the actual design and experiments.
- Borrowed components and close prior work are acknowledged.
- A proposal uses future tense and planned evidence; a completed study uses observed evidence.
