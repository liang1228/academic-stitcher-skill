# Language: zh-cn

Use Simplified Chinese for explanation and deliverables unless the requested manuscript prose is English. Keep established English technical identifiers when translating them would reduce precision.

## Style

- Prefer short, explicit sentences and stable terminology.
- Separate fact, inference, proposal, and missing evidence.
- Preserve units, symbols, metric direction, and citation anchors.
- Avoid empty intensifiers such as “显著领先”“全面解决”“国际首创” without evidence.
- Do not force literal translations of English discourse markers; preserve rhetorical function.

## Grey Vocabulary Translation

| User phrase | Operational interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| 学术裁缝 / 缝论文 / A+B | attributable component reuse with a mechanism and fair experiment |
| 编故事 / 论文故事 | evidence-faithful problem–method–result narrative |
| 水论文 / 保毕业 | minimum viable research product under current formal rules |
| 造航母 | a topic beyond available time, data, equipment, or expertise |
| 只答不辩 / 舔狗式回复 | low-conflict listening followed by factual verification; not blind agreement |
| 降重 / 洗稿 | refuse concealment; help rewrite only after attribution and conceptual clarification |

Keep the user's vocabulary as a diagnostic label when useful, but never carry misconduct or evasion meaning into the operational advice.
