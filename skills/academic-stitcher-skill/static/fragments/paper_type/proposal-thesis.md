# Paper Type: proposal-thesis

Use for opening reports, graduate theses, advisor-facing plans, graduation-focused papers, and defense preparation.

## Reader Question

Is the work feasible, attributable, coherent, and sufficient under current institutional and advisor requirements?

## Required Checks

- Current official graduation/assessment rule and target date.
- Advisor expectation and decision still needed.
- Stable research domain versus changeable method/module choices.
- Dataset, code, compute, participant, equipment, or field-access constraints.
- Authorship, lab-lineage, data, ethics, and permission boundaries.
- Minimum viable research product, evidence milestones, failure buffer, and allowed scope reduction.
- For a thesis, distinguish contribution units from workload units and show how chapters support one overall question.

Do not treat course anecdotes, prior cohorts, publication counts, or venue tiers as current policy.
