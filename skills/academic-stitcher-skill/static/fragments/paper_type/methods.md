# Paper Type: methods

Use for protocols, measurements, datasets, benchmarks, tools, or reusable technical procedures.

## Reader Question

Can another researcher understand, validate, and reuse the method within its declared scope?

## Required Evidence

- Defined input, output, procedure, and decision rules.
- Representative validation and comparison with current alternatives.
- Reliability, sensitivity, failure cases, and applicable uncertainty.
- Data/code/material availability, license, and reproducibility details.
- Boundary conditions and use cases supported by evidence.

Do not confuse a detailed procedure with a validated method.
