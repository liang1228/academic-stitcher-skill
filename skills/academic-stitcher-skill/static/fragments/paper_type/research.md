# Paper Type: research

Use for empirical findings, mechanisms, systems, interventions, or applied studies that are not better classified as algorithmic, methods, review, or proposal-thesis.

## Reader Question

What was found, why does it matter, and what evidence supports the interpretation?

## Required Chain

`problem -> unresolved gap -> study design -> result -> interpretation -> alternative explanation -> boundary`

Separate observation from mechanism. Preserve negative or unstable evidence that changes the conclusion. A useful bounded finding is preferable to an unsupported broad claim.
