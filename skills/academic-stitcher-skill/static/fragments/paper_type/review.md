# Paper Type: review

Use for literature reviews, survey sections, evidence maps, or state-of-field synthesis.

## Reader Question

What is known, how reliable is it, where do findings disagree, and what remains unresolved?

## Required Chain

`scope -> search/inclusion logic -> taxonomy -> evidence patterns -> disagreement/bias -> gap -> bounded implication`

- Organize by question or method family, not paper order.
- Separate primary evidence from secondary interpretation.
- Make missing, conflicting, and low-quality evidence visible.
- Do not use a review gap to declare a later method novel without checking close prior work.
