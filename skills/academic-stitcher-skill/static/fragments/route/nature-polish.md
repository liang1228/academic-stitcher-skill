# Route: nature-polish

Use when the user supplies prose and asks for structural editing, academic tone, concision, Chinese-English rewriting, or Nature-style clarity while preserving scientific meaning.

“Nature-style” means concise, reader-oriented, evidence-calibrated prose. It does not authorize invented significance, novelty, mechanisms, applications, or citations.

## Diagnosis Order

1. load `references/manuscript-control.md` when the edit changes structure,
   terminology, evidence placement, or spans multiple rounds;
2. paper-type logic;
3. section job and alignment checkpoint;
4. paragraph order and one-job discipline;
5. claim/evidence/boundary alignment;
6. terminology and source fidelity;
7. consistency sweep for numbers, claims, units, cross-references, and redundancy;
8. sentence clarity and style.

## Claim Calibration

- Direct controlled evidence: `show`, `demonstrate`, `estimate` as appropriate.
- Indirect or correlational evidence: `suggest`, `indicate`, `is consistent with`.
- Untested mechanism: `may`, `could`, `hypothesize`.
- Prediction only: `predict`, `is expected under`.

Remove or bound unsupported absolutes such as “first”, “unique”, “unprecedented”, “complete”, “always”, and “solves”. Preserve material limitations and counterevidence in visible, logically appropriate locations.

## Translation

- Preserve meaning, evidence state, terminology, units, and citation placement.
- Translate rhetorical function rather than idioms word-for-word.
- Do not use cross-language rewriting to conceal copying or evade similarity checks.
- If the source sentence is scientifically ambiguous, flag it instead of making the translation more specific.

## Contract

Use the `nature-polish` headings from `static/core/output-format.md`, including the process card. The `Change Log` must distinguish structural, claim-strength, terminology, and sentence-level changes.

## Boundary

- Improve organization, clarity, and calibrated wording without changing scientific meaning.
- Do not manufacture significance, novelty, mechanisms, applications, citations, or completed evidence.
- If a sentence cannot be made precise without new evidence, flag it and stop at the source-and-claim risk.
- Do not silently normalize a terminology or numeric mismatch. Record the
  observed variants and request the authoritative source when the distinction
  cannot be resolved from the supplied manuscript.
